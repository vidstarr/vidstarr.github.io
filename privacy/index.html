Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Introduction

VidStarr is a shoppable short video app where sellers post product videos and buyers watch, save, and shop. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. VidStarr is currently available in the United States.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, username, email address, and password when you create an account, and whether you joined as a buyer or a seller. Passwords are stored in encrypted form and we never see them.
  • Activity in the app: videos you watch, products you like, save, or add to your cart, and searches you make.
  • Content you post: comments you write, and for sellers, the product videos, titles, descriptions, and prices you upload.
  • Reports: if you report a comment, we keep the report so we can review it.

We do not collect payment card details, shipping addresses, or precise location at this time.

How we use your information

  • To run the app, keep you signed in, and show your saved products and cart.
  • To show view, like, and comment counts on products.
  • To connect buyers with sellers and display seller listings.
  • To review reported content and keep VidStarr safe.
  • To send account emails, such as password reset links.

What other people can see

Your username and any comments you post are visible to other VidStarr users. Seller product videos and listings are visible to everyone using the app. Your email address, saved products, and cart are private.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that host and run the app for us, such as Bubble, which stores our data and files, and only as needed to provide the service. We may also share information if required by law.

Data security

Information is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and stored with our hosting provider, which uses industry standard security. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: tap the profile icon, then Delete account. Your account, profile, comments, reports, saved products, and cart items are removed right away. Product listings are removed if you are a seller.

No longer have the app? Visit vidstarr.com/delete-account or email viddstarr@gmail.com, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing viddstarr@gmail.com.

Children

VidStarr is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VidStarr grows, for example when we add payments or shipping. We will change the date at the top, and let you know in the app if the changes are significant.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email viddstarr@gmail.com.
