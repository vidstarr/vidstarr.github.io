Delete your VidStarr account

You can delete your VidStarr account and the data linked to it at any time. Choose the option that works best for you.

Option 1 Fastest

Delete your account inside the VidStarr app:

  1. Open VidStarr and log in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are logged out.

Option 2

No longer have the app? Send us an email from the address linked to your VidStarr account with the subject Delete my account.

Email viddstarr@gmail.com

We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account and login details
  • Your profile information
  • Comments and reports you submitted
  • Items in your cart
  • Products and videos you listed, if you are a seller
  • Device information used for notifications

What we keep

Order and payment records are kept so we can handle payments, refunds, disputes, and tax and legal requirements. Your name and account are removed from these records when your account is deleted.

Payments on VidStarr are processed by Stripe, which keeps its own records under its privacy policy.
